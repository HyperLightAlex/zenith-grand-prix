Titan’s Rest · levels of detail

The exact three exported game models at all twenty Phase 3 viewpoints. Each row uses the same camera, framing, light and material palette. Open an image for its full resolution.

The game switches each part independently: LOD1 begins at 1.5 × its radius + 400 m, LOD2 at 4 × its radius + 1200 m. These close views deliberately expose simplification for review; nearby road-bearing parts remain at full detail in game. The far overview shows the across-course silhouette. The fixed road and white Z = 0 review sand are context only and are not exported.
