# The keyed pilots: what came back (Stage A0)

All fifteen, painted in ChatGPT on 6 November 2026 from `PROMPTS.md`.
`contact.png` has each beside its original; `teams.png` has each in all
five teams' colours (a preview, made by recolouring the key colours).

- They came back 1254 x 1254, not 1024: scaled when they are built.
- The framing matches the originals exactly (no shift or scale, measured).
- Recolouring touches only the suit and headset: the area the edit
  repainted, closed over the ink between panels and filled down to the
  bottom edge, plus every blue patch touching it (Solstice's blue is the
  key's blue, so its blue panels did not change). Hair, goggles, glasses,
  earrings and the like stay as painted.
- Small drift from the redraw: Bastion's hair highlight came back a brighter
  lavender than the original's blue-grey; Moth is softer than the rest;
  Sirocco's locs are redrawn a little. Outside the suit and headset the
  original can be used instead, since the framing matches.
- Lark's collar lining was blue and came back in the inner suit's dark key.
  Solstice's own colours map that key to its blue, so in Solstice it looks
  as before.
- Team emblems worn by a pilot: Squall's headband bolt and Anvil's cloud
  brooch (Tempest), Dune's scarab brooch (Mirage). To show only on their
  own team, each needs a version without it (`pilot-<name>-plain.png`):
  the emblem is then the difference, a layer of its own. Ibis's gold collar
  is part of her suit and takes each team's trim.

The template (`../trial/T1a` to `T1d`) passed: each edit changed only its
own area (compared pixel by pixel). Its face sits a little longer than the
layout guide (eyes higher, chin lower), which is fine since every part is
painted on it.
