# The keyed pilots: what came back (Stage A0)

All fifteen, painted in ChatGPT on 6 November 2026 from `PROMPTS.md`.
`contact.png` has each beside its original; `teams.png` has each in all
five teams' colours (a preview, made by recolouring the key colours).

- They came back 1254 x 1254, not 1024: scaled when they are built.
- The framing matches the originals exactly (no shift or scale, measured).
- Recolouring touches only the suit and headset: the area the edit
  repainted in the key colours, kept where it runs off the bottom edge (the
  suit) or holds several key colours together (the headset), so a strand of
  hair redrawn in a red close to the key's (Gale) is left alone; closed over
  the ink between panels and filled down to the bottom edge; plus every blue
  patch touching it (Solstice's blue is the key's blue, so its blue panels
  did not change). Hair, goggles, glasses, earrings and the like stay as
  painted.
- The green ground comes out with its edges un-mixed (each edge pixel
  re-blended from the nearest solid colour), so fine hair keeps no green or
  grey fringe.
- Each team's colours are taken from its own three pilots, except
  Solstice's inner suit, which is dark like everyone's (its pilots' blue
  linings are their own; straps like Orion's stay dark).
- On their own team a player gets the painting itself, unless it carries
  something taken out of the player's version.
- Small drift from the redraw: Bastion's hair highlight came back a brighter
  lavender than the original's blue-grey; Moth is softer than the rest;
  Sirocco's locs are redrawn a little. Outside the suit and headset the
  original can be used instead, since the framing matches.
- Lark's collar lining was blue and came back in the inner suit's dark key.
  On Solstice he is shown as painted, so it looks as before; on other teams
  it is their dark inner suit.
- Team emblems worn by a pilot: Squall's headband bolt (Tempest) is out of
  the player's version, on every team: `pilot-squall-plain.png`, painted out
  here (the headband's own white fitted over it); the computer Squall keeps
  it. Anvil's cloud brooch (Tempest) and Dune's scarab (Mirage) are still
  in, taking each team's trim colour; to take them out too, an edit each
  (PROMPTS-style: select the brooch, continue the suit behind it). Ibis's
  gold collar is part of her suit and takes each team's trim.

The template (`../trial/T1a` to `T1d`) passed: each edit changed only its
own area (compared pixel by pixel). Its face sits a little longer than the
layout guide (eyes higher, chin lower), which is fine since every part is
painted on it.
